About THAMIZHILHINDI

தமிழ் வழியில் எளிமையாக ஹிந்தி கற்போம்!

தமிழில் ஹிந்தி – தமிழ் மொழி வழியாக ஹிந்தியை மிக எளிமையாகவும் இயல்பாகவும் பேசக் கற்றுக்கொடுக்கும் ஒரு முதன்மையான பயிற்சி தளம்.

THAMIZHILHINDI (a dedicated language initiative by MATRIX MEDIA) helps learners understand and speak Hindi effortlessly through Tamil. Founded on our teacher's 30+ years of dedicated Hindi teaching experience, our mission is to eliminate the hesitation of learning a new language through proven, mother-tongue-based explanations.

We provide comprehensive Online and Offline learning opportunities designed to meet different learner needs — including school and college students, working professionals communicating across pan-India offices, homemakers, and absolute beginners starting from zero.

Our training replaces heavy grammatical rules with clear, simple explanations and everyday conversations. By connecting Hindi vocabulary directly with familiar Tamil meanings and pronunciations, learners easily understand sentences and speak with natural fluency.
