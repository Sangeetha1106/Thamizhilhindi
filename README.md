## 📁 Website Pages & Architecture

- **`index.html`** – Full-width edge-to-edge promotional banner, interactive action buttons, admissions card, 4 learning highlights, course previews, and direct contact options.
- **`about.html`** – Founder's 30+ years of teaching background, **Why Choose Us** (4 feature cards), and **Our Teaching Methods** (4-step visual timeline).
- **`courses.html`** – 6 structured Hindi course options:
  1. Basic Hindi
  2. Intermediate Hindi
  3. Advanced Hindi – Reading & Writing
  4. Exclusive Reading & Writing
  5. Online & Offline Learning
  6. Special Workshops (3 Hours, 1 Day, 3 Days, 5 Days)
- **`learn-hindi.html`** – 12 essential daily vocabulary cards (Devanagari, Tamil pronunciation, Tamil meaning, English), 2-person daily conversation dialogue bubbles, and visual learning benefits.
- **`contact.html`** – Admission enquiry form, instant WhatsApp chat button, and direct call actions.
- **`style.css`** – Modern responsive stylesheet with zero dark backgrounds.

## 🚀 How to Enable GitHub Pages (Free Live Hosting)

To host this website live directly from this repository:
1. Go to your repository on GitHub: `https://github.com/Sangeetha1106/Thamizhilhindi`
2. Click **Settings** (top menu bar)
3. In the left sidebar, click **Pages**
4. Under **Build and deployment > Branch**:
   - Select **`main`**
   - Select folder **`/ (root)`**
   - Click **Save**
5. Within 1–2 minutes, your website will be live at:
   `https://sangeetha1106.github.io/Thamizhilhindi/`
